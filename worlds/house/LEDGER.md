# THE MYSTERY LEDGER — THE GREAT INDOORS

Same rules as the vale's ledger: noticed never assigned; answers resolve
into story or charm; NEVER rows are permanent and load-bearing; the tithe
is audited across all questions at milestone *The Tithe*.

| # | The question | Where the answer lives |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the Blinkbox saying? | **NEVER.** Three short, two long, one short. Same as always. The vigil continues. |
| 2 | What is inside the littlest dollhouse — the one inside the Dollhouse, door ajar? | **NEVER.** The atlas's deepest kept door. You can lean. You cannot enter. |
| 3 | Where did the Human learn the half-phrase They whistle at the sink? | **NEVER.** The ants already know the tune. Nobody can say why They do. |
| 4 | Is there anything in the rug fringe? | **NEVER.** There is definitely nothing in the fringe. Go on then. You go. |
| 5 | Is the Backwards Town real? | Unruled, on purpose: go press your hand to theirs and decide. The elders' debate must never be settled by us. |
| 6 | Where do the odd socks go? | Vera takes ONE a year, for wool. The dryer eats the rest's reputations. The mitten evidence is everywhere and nobody has done the math. Solving this is pure joy. |
| 7 | Does the Dog do it on purpose? | Watch long enough: the Dog looks straight at you and drops a crumb. Confirmed ally. The wink is canon. |
| 8 | Why is there a spoon in the honey jar, and why does nobody remove it? | Since the Wedding. Don't ask Them; They argue. (Muffled-music argument, twice a year, weirdly fond.) |
| 9 | Why do They keep the broken lamp? | Each thinks the other loves it. The ants worked this out generations ago and consider it the definitive fact about Humans. |
| 10 | Why no Eruption on Sundays? | Pancakes. The evidence is a smell and a lie-in. |
| 11 | What is the Bright, really? | The window crystal splits the sun; the patch walks with the day. One ant knows. "I've SEEN the crystal!" Answered — and disbelieved, which is funnier. |
| 12 | Are the stamps portraits of ancestors? | They're stamps. One ant knows this too. He and the crystal witness have formed a support group of two. |
| 13 | What happened to Aunt Tessie? | Napped on the warm rectangle, got "texted to Denmark," came back by post. She's FINE. She gives talks. |
| 14 | Do dust bunnies eat? | Never observed eating. Never observed hungry. The herd endures. (Held open — a soft never unless the build finds something kinder.) |
| 15 | What is Drainton? | Folklore of the Deep. The third rung down is the agreed edge of the world, and the ledger respects town law. (Folklore stays folklore.) |
| 16 | Where is Rove? | The attic, working. Pages down the vent on warm days; sprinkle lines all bend toward the hatch. |
| 17 | What did the ants spell, the morning the Humans found "hi" on the screen? | *(Reserved. The answer will be a player, some night, with friends, one tile each.)* |
| 18 | What does the Wand's disappearance mean for ant–Human relations? | Under the couch, third cushion span. The town knows. The debate ("return it? how? WHY?") is a standing item at the matchbox hall, and it is beautiful. |

Tally at opening: 18 questions · 12 answered · 4 NEVER (#1, #2, #3, #4)
· #5 unruled on principle · #17 reserved. The famous four are famous
because they are the tithe; the one-in-ten runs across all questions,
micro included, audited at *The Tithe*.
