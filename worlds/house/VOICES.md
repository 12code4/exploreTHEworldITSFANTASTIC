Ruled by the root [DIALOGUE.md](../../DIALOGUE.md). Text boxes only: one
or two short sentences, one bit per person, loud is allowed.

- "As mayor, I can confirm the toast WILL erupt at 7:40."
- "The Thimble predates the town. Possibly the House. As mayor."
- "Rule 3 exists for a reason. The reason is Vacuum Day."
- *(follow-up)* "There are no other rules worth having."
- "Welcome to the Great Indoors! Mind the gap in Rule 3."
