# THE COLOR SCRIPT — THE GREAT INDOORS

Same laws as everywhere (see the vale's [COLOR.md](../vale/COLOR.md) for
the doctrine): **color always has a source** and **saturation is a
gesture.** The House's sources are modern — appliance light, screen glow,
LED points, sun through a hanging crystal — which gives this page a
palette no valley could own.

## The keys

### Key of Grain — the ground truth
Wood floors, honey light, domestic warmth. The House's Moss.
`#B98F5A` floorboard · `#8A6844` skirting · `#E8DCC8` wall milk ·
`#C4B49A` carpet oat · `#F2E4C2` lamp warm · `#5A4632` shadow wood

### Key of Chrome — the kitchen register
Steel, tile, morning glare. Clean and bright, never cold-hearted.
`#D8DEE2` steel · `#F4F6F4` tile · `#AEBDC4` shadow chrome ·
`#F6E8B0` butter light · `#7C9CA8` sink water

### Key of Sugar — the pastel detonation
The sprinkle routes, the spilled sprinkle scree in the pantry corner,
the birthday shelf, the sugar mine glittering.
`#F4B8C8` rose sprinkle · `#A8D8E8` blue sprinkle · `#B8E8B0` mint ·
`#F6E19A` lemon · `#D8B8E8` violet · `#FFF8E8` sugar white

### Key of Glow — the night house
Not dark: a LED constellation. Every appliance a colored star.
`#1A1E24` house night · `#2A3A55` moon spill · `#58C878` router green ·
`#E84E4E` standby red · `#78D8E8` charger white-blue ·
`#88E8A0` microwave clock · `#4A6AE8` the Screen's Blue Hour wash

### Key of the Bright — the prism, migrating
The sun-catcher's rainbow patch, crawling the floor with the sun; the
bathroom on bubble night; the CD spindle's spilled spectrum.
Day (soft): `#FF9AA2 #FFC48C #FFF3A0 #B8E8A0 #9AD8E8 #A8B4F0 #D8A8E8`
Bubble night (jewel, wet): `#E84E6A #F0A03C #F2D848 #58B87A #4A9AD8 #7A6AE0`

### Key of Cold — the Opening
The fridge aurora: rolling fog and shelf-light.
`#DFF2F6` fog · `#B8E0E8` cold blue · `#FFF6D8` shelf gold ·
`#98C8C0` crisper green

## The ten postcards

1. **The Opening** — fridge fog rolling over three small silhouettes,
   shelf-light gold behind.
2. **The Bright mid-carpet** — a rainbow patch on oat weave, one ant
   standing in it, arms out.
3. **The Blue Hour** — the bookshelf ledge lined with tiny watchers,
   the room all screen-blue.
4. **The LED constellation** — the dark kitchen as a star field:
   green, red, white-blue, the microwave's patient clock.
5. **The Meteor** — mail mid-fall, huge and white, the hall in
   letterbox light.
6. **The steam jungle** — tile cliffs in fog, one towel-rail fern
   uncurling.
7. **Sprinkle line orange** — a dotted candy route vanishing under the
   fridge into the dark.
8. **Vacuum Day aftermath** — the terrible clean plains, and one
   defiant crumb.
9. **The dust-bunny herd** — grey shapes drifting through the one
   sunbeam under the bed.
10. **The Dollhouse, first sight** — sheet hills, one small window's
    light, a door ajar at exactly your scale.

(The eleventh is the knot-hole with Outside inking green through it,
and it is not listed, for the usual reason.)
