# exploreTHEworldITSFANTASTIC

> **IF A WORLD IS FANTASTIC, IT'S EXPLORED.**

An exploration game built on one fable and nothing else. No markers, no
checklists, no bribes — a world with light, life, charm, and small mysteries,
and the bet that a world worth exploring never has to ask.

**Status:** the world does not exist yet, on purpose. Worlds built before
their philosophy come out as content. So first, the philosophy was ironed
flat:

### → [PHILOSOPHY.md](PHILOSOPHY.md)

What "fantastic" is made of (six materials), the Ten Laws it forces on the
build, the Forbidden list, the Covenant with the player, and the Tests that
keep the whole thing honest.

The world comes next. It answers to that file.
